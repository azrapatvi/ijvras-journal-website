Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about submitting, reviewing, and publishing your research in IJVRAS.

Home FAQ
General Questions
1
What is IJVRAS?
International Journal of Versatile Research & Analytical Studies (IJVRAS) is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal that publishes high-quality research in engineering, science, management, and multidisciplinary fields. It is committed to disseminating original and impactful scholarly work to researchers worldwide.
2
Is IJVRAS a peer-reviewed journal?
Yes, all submitted manuscripts undergo a rigorous peer-review process by qualified reviewers before acceptance. We follow a double-blind peer review model to ensure unbiased and objective evaluation of every submission.
3
What is the review time?
Our average review time is 24–48 hours, depending on reviewer availability and manuscript quality. We are committed to providing fast, efficient, and thorough peer review without compromising on the integrity of the process.
4
Is the journal open access?
Yes, IJVRAS is a fully open-access journal, meaning all published articles are freely available to readers worldwide — with no subscription or access fees. This maximizes the visibility and impact of your published research.
Submission & Fees
6
What is the publication fee?
IJVRAS charges a nominal publication fee to cover processing, peer review. There is no submission fee — you only pay after your paper has been accepted. Please visit our Fee page for detailed pricing information.
7
How can I submit my paper?
Authors can submit their manuscripts through our online submission system (OJS platform). Simply register or log in to your account, follow the submission guidelines, and upload your manuscript. Our editorial team will acknowledge receipt and guide you through the review process.
8
What types of papers do you accept?
We welcome a variety of scholarly contributions, including:
  • Research Papers
  • Review Papers
  • Case Studies
  • Technical Papers
Policies & Publication
9
Is plagiarism checked?
Yes, all submissions are checked using plagiarism detection tools before proceeding to peer review. Papers with high similarity scores will be rejected immediately. Authors are advised to ensure originality and properly cite all referenced works.
10
Can I track my paper status?
Yes, authors can track their submission status through the online system after login. You will receive email notifications at each stage of the review and publication process to keep you informed.
11
Do you provide certificates?
Yes, publication certificates are provided to authors after successful publication. These certificates serve as official proof of your published work and can be used for academic or professional purposes.
12
Can I submit a paper that is already published?
No, IJVRAS only accepts original and unpublished work. Simultaneous submission to another journal is also not permitted. Manuscripts that have been previously published elsewhere, in whole or in part, will be rejected without review.
13
What is the acceptance rate?
Acceptance depends on the quality, originality, and reviewer feedback for each paper. Only high-quality papers that meet our editorial standards are accepted. We maintain a selective acceptance process to uphold the journal's academic integrity and reputation.
14
Do you support multiple authors?
Yes, multi-author submissions are allowed, and all authors must provide proper details including name, affiliation, and email address. The corresponding author is responsible for communication with the journal throughout the review and publication process.

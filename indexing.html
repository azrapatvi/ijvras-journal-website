Indexing

IJVRAS is indexed in leading academic databases, ensuring global visibility for every published article.

IJVRAS is committed to making published research widely accessible and discoverable. Our journal is currently indexed in Google Scholar and ScienceDirect — two of the most trusted and widely used academic platforms in the world — ensuring that your research reaches readers and researchers globally.
Current Indexing

Google Scholar

Free academic search engine by Google

Google Scholar is a free search engine that anyone can use to find research papers, articles, and journals. All articles published in IJVRAS are listed on Google Scholar, so researchers from any country can easily find and cite your work.

Actively Indexed Visit Google Scholar

ScienceDirect

Leading scientific database by Elsevier

ScienceDirect is one of the biggest and most respected databases for scientific and research articles in the world. It is run by Elsevier and is used by universities, libraries, and researchers everywhere. IJVRAS articles indexed on ScienceDirect get more visibility, more readers, and more citations — helping your research make a bigger impact.

Publication Process

Simple step-by-step guide — from writing your paper to getting it published in IJVRAS.

The entire process from submission to publication usually takes 2–5 days. Our team is available to help you at every step. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.

1
Write & Prepare Your Paper
Before submitting, make sure your research paper is ready and properly formatted.
Write your paper in English
Use our official paper template (Word format)
Include: Title, Abstract, Keywords, Introduction, Methodology, Results, Conclusion, References
Check for plagiarism before submitting (should be below 15%)
Download Template
2
Submit Your Paper Online
Once your paper is ready, submit it through our online system. It is quick and easy.
Register or log in at ijvras.com
Fill in the paper details (title, authors, abstract)
Upload your paper file (PDF or Word)
You will get a confirmation email after submission
Submit Now
3
Initial Check by Editor
Our editor first checks your paper to make sure it meets basic requirements.
Checks if topic matches the journal's scope
Checks formatting and plagiarism
This takes about 1–2 days
If something is wrong, we will email you to fix it
4
Peer Review
Your paper is sent to 2 expert reviewers in your field. They read it carefully and give their opinion.
Double-blind review — reviewers don't know your name, you don't know theirs
Usually takes 24–48 hours
Result: Accepted / Minor Revision / Major Revision / Rejected
If revision needed, you'll get reviewer comments by email
5
Revision (If Required)
If reviewers ask for changes, you need to update your paper and resubmit.
Read the reviewer comments carefully
Make the requested changes in your paper
Submit the revised paper within the given deadline
Write a short note explaining what changes you made
6
Pay Publication Fee (After Acceptance Only)
You only pay AFTER your paper is accepted. There is NO submission fee.
No fee if your paper is rejected
Fee details will be shared in the acceptance email
Payment can be made online easily
View Fee Details
7
Paper Published! 🎉
Congratulations! Your paper is now live on IJVRAS and visible to the whole world.
Your paper gets a DOI (Digital Object Identifier) — a permanent link
Indexed in Google Scholar and Science Direct
You receive a digital certificate of publication
Free to read for anyone, anywhere in the world
